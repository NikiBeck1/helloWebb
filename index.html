Frequently Asked Questions

What makes helloWebb different from other web designers? +
We're not just web designers—we're full-stack developers and creative professionals. With 10+ years of experience using professional tools like Figma, Adobe Creative Suite, and Replit, we bring enterprise-level expertise to local businesses. We combine custom development, brand identity design, photography, videography, and AI-powered automation that most agencies don't offer. Instead of just building a pretty website, we create complete digital systems using GoHighLevel and Search Atlas that automate your SEO, lead capture, and client communication. Plus, we specialize in local businesses (0-50 employees) and price our services fairly—no corporate markup or hidden fees.
How long does it take to build my website? +
Timeline depends on the package you choose. Starter Sites typically take 1-2 weeks, Standard Sites take 2-3 weeks, and Pro Sites with advanced automation, custom development, and professional content creation take 3-4 weeks. Unlike traditional agencies that take months, our streamlined workflow and AI-enhanced tools let us deliver professional results faster without compromising quality. We'll give you a specific timeline during your free consultation based on your unique needs.
Do you offer ongoing support after my website launches? +
Absolutely! All packages include initial training and support. Standard Sites include 1 month of post-launch support, and Pro Sites include 3 months of ongoing optimization. After that, we offer flexible monthly maintenance plans for updates, content changes, SEO monitoring, and technical support. We're here to help your business succeed long-term—not just hand you a website and disappear. You'll always be able to reach us when you need assistance.
What exactly is AI-powered SEO automation? +
Traditional SEO requires constant manual updates—fixing broken links, updating meta tags, optimizing images, and monitoring rankings. Our Search Atlas integration uses OTTO AI to automatically detect and fix these issues in real-time. It's like having a full-time SEO specialist working on your site 24/7. The system monitors your rankings, analyzes competitors, optimizes your content, and makes technical improvements automatically—typically saving business owners 10+ hours per week on tasks that would otherwise require a specialist.
Do you provide photography and video services? +
Yes! We offer professional photography and videography as part of our Pro Site package or as standalone services. We capture authentic photos and videos of your business, team, and work—no cheesy stock imagery. This includes product photography, team headshots, facility tours, and promotional videos. We handle everything from shooting to editing using professional Adobe Creative Suite tools, giving you high-quality content that actually represents your brand and connects with your local audience.

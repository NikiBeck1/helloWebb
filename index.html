Questions

How can you charge so little?

My AI-assisted process cuts out the slow, repetitive parts of design and development. That time savings goes to you as a lower price — every page still gets reviewed and refined by me.

How fast is “fast”?

Most single-page sites launch within 3 days and multi-page sites within 7 days, depending on how quickly we trade feedback and content.

Platform or custom code — which should I choose?

If you want to edit the site yourself, a platform is the easiest route. If you want the lowest monthly cost and are happy to send me changes, custom code with my hosting is the better deal.

What counts as a custom project?

Online stores, custom features, integrations with other tools, full brand identity work, or larger sites. I'll send a clear quote before any work begins.
